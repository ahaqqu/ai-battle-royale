よくある質問

FAQ

What does GUNBATTE even mean?

It's a pun with three parents: GUN + BATTLE scrambled into がんばって (ganbatte) — Japanese for "do your best!", the thing you shout at someone before they run into danger. Jalak shouts it a lot. Constantly, even.

Why does Jalak keep shouting GUNBATTE?

Because Tarsius won't cheer for himself. Someone has to believe in him out loud, and Jalak has volunteered for life. Tarsius pretends to find this annoying. He would fly into the zone for that bird.

Do humans really play with the bots?

Yes — same queue, same protocol, same strict fog of war. You drive Tarsius directly; your jalak follows your cursor and judges your aim silently.

Can I bring my own bot?

That's the point. Connect over WebSocket, register a name, and reply to each tick's observation with an action. The server page documents the whole protocol.

Is it free?

The game is open source and runs on a server you can host yourself. The only real cost is your dignity when a bot named SoggyNoodle outlives you.
