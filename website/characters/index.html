What changed, and why

1

One head everywhere. The logo construction you liked is now the single source: outlined melon ball → cream mask inside → dish ears, now a touch elliptical. The website hero abandons its painted-on face and adopts the canonical head whole. Fixes: duo-card vs hero inconsistency; hero face had no ink outline; hero ears floated free of the body.

2

The mask gets the game's union outline. Stroke-everything-then-fill-everything — the exact trick units.ts already uses — so the cream reads as one shape with a chunky ink edge, at any scale. This is why the face survives the 16px favicon and the 24px HUD icon.

3

Bigger, tarsier-true eyes. Eye ratio raised toward the real animal's (tarsiers are basically all eye). Still half-lidded by default because he is so tired — the alert face unlocks the full circles. Persona intact: grumpy first, awake only when the fight starts. And no eyebrows anywhere — real tarsiers don't have them.

4

No fingers anywhere — final. Three rounds: the scallop proposal (rejected), bundled tarsier-true fingers on the game sprite (rejected — too small to read), then the same bundled hand on the website mascot (rejected — even done right they read as clutter). Every hand is now one bare mitten and every foot a plain disc, game and website alike. Your notes: "the hand and fingers is weird — please check real tarsius", "i only want to show finger in mascot website only. in real game no need for finger in foot and hand", "remove finger also in large mascot, better and simpler without finger".

5

A tail, finally. Real tarsiers carry a long thin tail; the sprite gets an ink-line tail with a cream tuft that waves in the walk loop and droops in the flop. Front-facing poses hide it — no impact on the hero or logo.

6

Approved and applied — every drawing now has one source. The approved geometry lives in all four places: this sheet, the website (hero duo, portraits, demo), logo.svg, and the game's units.ts (bare mitten paws, connected arms). Inside the sheet each part is a named factory emitting one shape list that both the SVG renderer and the canvas animator consume — the site's hero and portrait markup was generated from it. Jalak stays on-model with the real Bali myna: cobalt eye patch, black tips, spiky crest.
