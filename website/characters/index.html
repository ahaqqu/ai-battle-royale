What changed, and why

1

One head everywhere. The logo construction you liked is now the single source: outlined melon ball → cream mask inside → dish ears. The website hero abandons its floating unoutlined face and adopts it. Fixes: duo-card vs hero inconsistency; hero face had no ink outline.

2

The mask gets the game's union outline. Stroke-everything-then-fill-everything — the exact trick units.ts already uses — so the cream reads as one shape with a chunky ink edge, at any scale. This is why the face survives the 16px favicon and the 24px HUD icon.

3

Bigger, tarsier-true eyes. Eye ratio raised toward the real animal's (tarsiers are basically all eye). Still half-lidded by default because he is so tired — the alert face unlocks the full circles. Persona intact: grumpy first, awake only when the fight starts.

4

Hands reworked. Flat dot-mittens → a palm with three finger scallops and a thumb line, wrapped around the blaster when armed, relaxed when not. Your note: "head in game is okay but the hand seems weird" — this is that fix, propagated to every scenario.

5

A tail, finally. Real tarsiers carry a long thin tail; the sprite gets an ink-line tail with a cream tuft that waves in the walk loop and droops in the flop. Front-facing poses hide it — no impact on the hero or logo.

6

Nothing existing is touched. This file is standalone; index.html, logo.svg and units.ts are byte-identical. If you approve, the same geometry back-ports into those three places. Jalak stays on-model with the real Bali myna: cobalt eye patch, black tips, spiky crest.
